# Applying learned research techniques to a project

Select and apply techniques learned from research to a project's concrete
problem and constraints. This is a distinct project requirement, not an alias
for understanding a paper or adapting its implementation.

## Projects needing this skill

- [Latent MAS](../projects/agents-latent-mas.md) — user-confirmed project requirement.
- [Janus Multivariate Time-Series Transformers and Predictive Modeling](../projects/janus-mvts-transformer-predictive-modeling.md) — user-confirmed project requirement.
- [Janus Deep RL for Industrial Process Optimization and Visualization](../projects/janus-rl-process-optimization-and-visualization.md) — user-confirmed project requirement.
- [Janus Reinforcement-Learning Training and Exploration](../projects/janus-rl-training-and-exploration.md) — user-confirmed project requirement.
- [Janus Latent Constraints: Controlled Generation for Images and Tabular Data](../projects/janus-latent-constraints-controlled-generation.md) — user-confirmed research-to-implementation requirement for tabular data.

## Projects with user-reported use

- None recorded.

## Projects showing this skill

- [Latent MAS](../projects/agents-latent-mas.md) — the source states that the LatentMAS paper implementation was replicated with Qwen and was being extended to the team's use case ([slide 22](../sources/agents-latent-mas-20260112-latentmas-v01-pptx.md#slide-22)); it does not assign that work to an individual.
- [Janus Multivariate Time-Series Transformers and Predictive Modeling](../projects/janus-mvts-transformer-predictive-modeling.md) — the presentation describes adapting a published Transformer framework to multivariate time-series regression and pretraining; it does not establish who performed the adaptation ([paper](../sources/janus-mvts-20240812-janus-p2-mvts-v01-pptx.md#slide-29); [architecture](../sources/janus-mvts-20240812-janus-p2-mvts-v01-pptx.md#slide-30)).
- [Janus Deep RL for Industrial Process Optimization and Visualization](../projects/janus-rl-process-optimization-and-visualization.md) — the paper applies RL methods and visual-analysis techniques to a rubber-mix process problem; it does not assign those choices to an individual ([setup](../sources/janus-publication-rl-visualizations-pdf.md#problem-setup); [methods](../sources/janus-publication-rl-visualizations-pdf.md#visualization-methods)).
- [Janus Reinforcement-Learning Training and Exploration](../projects/janus-rl-training-and-exploration.md) — the presentation considers research methods for manufacturing control and an external simulator as potential reference approaches; it does not establish an individual implementation of those methods ([RL papers](../sources/janus-rl-20230418-janus-paperreview-v10-pptx.md#slides-2-5); [beer simulator](../sources/janus-rl-20230418-janus-paperreview-v10-pptx.md#slides-6-7)).

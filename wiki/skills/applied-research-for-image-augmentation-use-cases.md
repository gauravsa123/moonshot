# Applied research for image-augmentation use cases

Investigate whether image-generation and augmentation methods address a
specific data or model problem, and design experiments to assess their
usefulness.

## Projects showing this skill

- [GAN Publication: Advanced GAN for Tire-Defect Augmentation](../projects/gan-publication-advanced-gan-for-tire-defect-augmentation.md) — the paper describes experiments applying GAN augmentation to a limited tire-joint dataset and downstream image classification; the reported results are not independently validated ([experiments](../sources/gan-publication-gan-paper-formatted-2-pdf.md#experiments-and-results)).
- [GAN Exploration, Image Synthesis, and Data Augmentation](../projects/gan-exploration-image-synthesis-and-augmentation.md) — the sources describe applied GAN experiments for tire-conicity detection and tire-joint image augmentation; reported findings are not independently validated ([conicity experiment](../sources/gan-exploration-20200717-gan-study-v3-pptx.md#slide-50); [tire-joint experiments](../sources/gan-exploration-ai-moonshot-gan-09122021-pptx.md#slide-8)).
- [GAN for IRIS2 Multimodal Tire Imaging](../projects/gan-iris2-multimodal-tire-imaging.md) — the deck compares IRIS2 image-generation variants with different training and architecture modifications; visual improvement claims remain unverified ([slide 25](../sources/gan-iris2-20210401-gan-iris2-v1-pptx.md#slide-25)).
- [GAN KAR Hackathon: Tire-Joint Defect Augmentation](../projects/gan-kar-tire-joint-defect-augmentation.md) — the deck describes comparisons between per-defect and OK-image training variants for the augmentation task ([slide 24](../sources/gan-kar-20210310-gan-hackathon-kar-v1-pptx.md#slide-24)).
- [GAN Hormiga: Road-Image Generation and Enhancement](../projects/gan-hormiga-road-image-generation-and-enhancement.md) — the deck compares direct GAN generation, CycleGAN transfer, and image-enhancement options, but gives no quantitative downstream evaluation ([approaches](../sources/gan-hormiga-20210820-gan-hormiga-v1-pptx.md#slide-2); [enhancement alternatives](../sources/gan-hormiga-20210820-gan-hormiga-v1-pptx.md#slide-7)).

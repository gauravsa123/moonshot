# Evaluation of generated-image quality and similarity

Assess generated-image quality and compare generated samples with target or
training data using suitable evaluation measures.

## Projects showing this skill

- [GAN Publication: Advanced GAN for Tire-Defect Augmentation](../projects/gan-publication-advanced-gan-for-tire-defect-augmentation.md) — the paper reports FID comparisons between StyleGAN and its Advanced GAN; values and figures have not been validated ([FID comparison](../sources/gan-publication-gan-paper-formatted-2-pdf.md#fid-comparisons)).
- [Conditional Diffusion Models for Image Generation](../projects/gan-diffusion-models-for-image-generation.md) — the deck shows result categories and proposes cross-correlation similarity, but the method is marked for future implementation and the images were not visually inspected ([slides 8](../sources/gan-diffusion-20230324-diffusion-v01-pptx.md#slide-8), [10](../sources/gan-diffusion-20230324-diffusion-v01-pptx.md#slide-10)).
- [GAN Exploration, Image Synthesis, and Data Augmentation](../projects/gan-exploration-image-synthesis-and-augmentation.md) — the decks report FID comparisons and Inception Score as image-generation evaluation methods; the values and visuals have not been independently validated ([V5 FID](../sources/gan-exploration-20200717-gan-study-v5-pptx.md#slide-28); [FID](../sources/gan-exploration-20200717-gan-study-v3-pptx.md#slide-34); [quality measures](../sources/gan-exploration-ai-moonshot-gan-01072021-pptx.md#slide-11)).
- [GAN for IRIS2 Multimodal Tire Imaging](../projects/gan-iris2-multimodal-tire-imaging.md) — the presentation labels visual comparisons of generated images and detail, but extracted text does not describe an objective quality metric and the samples remain uninspected ([slide 23](../sources/gan-iris2-20210401-gan-iris2-v1-pptx.md#slide-23)).
- [GAN KAR Hackathon: Tire-Joint Defect Augmentation](../projects/gan-kar-tire-joint-defect-augmentation.md) — the deck lists FID values, but the text does not explain the comparison and the results have not been validated ([slide 3](../sources/gan-kar-20210310-gan-hackathon-kar-v1-pptx.md#slide-3)).

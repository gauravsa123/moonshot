## Summary

The deck frames a Safer Roads service for systemic network-wide risk analysis,
including road locations with no prior crash history but with similar roadway,
user-volume, or environmental risk characteristics. It describes using
accidents and near-miss events as evidence and integrating road/POI context
with multimodal data to infer where VRU risk may be higher ([slides 3–5](#slide-3)).

The work summary lists accident and event records, road cartography, POI data,
and VRU/non-VRU labels. It describes clustering arcs, interpreting VRU-related
patterns, extracting similar arcs, counting events, and producing map
visualizations and possible risk outputs ([slides 6–8](#slide-6)).

## Slide references

### Slide 3

The slide defines a systemic approach as network-wide, data-driven
identification of roadway features associated with crash types and notes that
some accidents occur at new locations.

### Slide 4

The deck discusses near-misses involving VRUs and the challenge that captured
events may not identify user involvement. It proposes using location facts and
patterns to infer likely VRU involvement.

### Slide 5

The roadmap includes data preparation, AOI selection, clustering, event
inference, cluster characterization, pipeline building, and potential go-live.
These roadmap stages are not treated as completed deliverables.

### Slide 6

The work summary lists regions, accident/event records, POI/road features,
clustering, cluster interpretation, feature importance, event counts, and
potential VRU-risk outputs.

### Slide 7

The pipeline includes accident/POI data, deep clustering, MCA, autoencoding,
UMAP, relative-frequency analysis, visualization, and similarity evaluation.

### Slide 8

The slide describes inferring possible VRU involvement in events from patterns
in POIs, land use, traffic characteristics, and time.

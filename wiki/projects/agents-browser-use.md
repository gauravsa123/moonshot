# Browser Use and Enterprise Web Automation

## Project summary

The sources cover related but distinct stages of enterprise web automation. The 2025 AgentTaskX material describes a planner/executor design for enterprise and open websites: enterprise documentation can be retrieved with RAG, clickable controls are grounded through bounding-box IDs linked to XPath, and a restricted action set and reflection are used during browser execution. The slide deck also includes an RPA Agent wrapper built around the `browser-use` project, plus retailer-analysis and employee-workflow examples. The later ICAART-2026-named paper focuses on that wrapper as an interface for broader use: prompt/configuration inputs, Generate and Replay modes, content extraction, optional evaluation, and a retailer competition-analysis use case. The papers describe related work, but the AgentTaskX architecture and the `browser-use`-based RPA Agent should not be treated as the same implementation.

Dates and results are kept source-specific: the deck filename contains `20251016` and slide 8 labels a publication “MLDS 2025”; the 2025 AgentTaskX paper is separately identified in the later paper’s references as a 2025 work published on 11 February 2025 ([later paper, p. 7](../sources/agents-browser-use-conference-rpa-icaart-2026-final-pdf.md#page-7)); the third file is labeled ICAART 2026 in its filename. The AgentTaskX material reports a 24% improvement associated with RAG for internal webpages and discusses a 40% end-to-end benchmark, while the later wrapper paper compares estimated task times with conventional RPA. These are different reported measures and contexts, not interchangeable results. The sources name authors but do not assign individual responsibilities. No personal role, contribution, or mastery is inferred.

Choose the wiki's foundation

All three keep raw/ immutable and support project-by-project ingestion. The difference is what becomes the canonical knowledge layer.

A

Markdown-first

Knowledge flow
raw files → linked Markdown pages → Obsidian graph view

Most direct match to the gist; easiest to read and maintain. Structured skill analysis and reliable future charts may need extra conventions later.

B

Graph-first

Knowledge flow
raw files → typed entities + relationships → generated pages and visual map

Relationships and skill maps are explicit from day one. Requires more schema/tooling, and the graph is less pleasant as the primary reading and editing surface.

C

Markdown canonical, graph as a view Recommended

Knowledge flow
raw files → cited Markdown + consistent metadata → graph, map, portfolio when needed

Readable, agent-maintainable wiki stays authoritative; small structured fields make future skills maps and portfolio views reproducible without building those outputs now.
